## 4. AP2 Protocol Monetization

### How We Make Money from AP2

1. **Transaction Fees**: Charge $0.10-$0.25 per automated expense payment
2. **Subscription Tiers**: Higher tiers include more AP2 transactions
3. **Agent Services**: Charge for AI-powered expense approval automation
4. **Mandate Management**: Premium feature for cryptographic authorization

### AP2 Value Proposition

**For Customers**:
- Automated expense approvals with cryptographic verification
- Fraud-proof audit trails (Intent → Cart → Payment mandates)
- Reduces manual approval time by 80%
- Multi-payment method support (cards, crypto, bank transfers)

**For Us (Revenue)**:
- Transaction fees on every AP2 payment
- Premium tier requirement for AP2 access
- Add-on services for advanced automation
