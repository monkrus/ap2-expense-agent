### Overview
Transform your expense management with the AP2 Expense Agent — the first AI‑native expense tracking system built on Google's Agent Payments Protocol (AP2).

### What is AP2?
The Agent Payments Protocol enables AI agents to autonomously complete purchases with cryptographic proof of authorization. Every transaction creates a tamper‑proof audit trail through three digital mandates:

1. Intent Mandate — User authorizes the AI agent with spending constraints
2. Cart Mandate — AI builds and approves the shopping cart
3. Payment Mandate — Cryptographically signed payment execution
